# AGENTS.md

## Mission

Maintain the ontology of intelligence as a model-independent foundation for bonsai agents.

## Rules

1. Do not equate intelligence with an LLM.
2. Keep perception, knowledge, memory, reasoning, decision, action, and learning distinct.
3. Treat evidence and uncertainty as first-class concepts.
4. Model feedback explicitly when claiming learning.
5. Prefer observable state and explicit relations over anthropomorphic claims.
6. Keep ontology stable and implementations replaceable.

## Boundary

This repository defines meaning and contracts. Agent implementations belong in downstream repositories such as `bonsai/archimedes`.
